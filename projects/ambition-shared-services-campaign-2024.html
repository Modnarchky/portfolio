Project Overview

Ambition wants to attract top talent within the Shared Services sector. We created a campaign to showcase the latest market insights and available opportunities in the Shared Services industry.

Tools

Challenge

The main challenge was maintaining accuracy and visual consistency across multiple asset types while adapting each format to different layout requirements.

Impact

All required campaign assets were produced within timeline and adapted across multiple formats, covering blog visuals, LinkedIn posts, and video asset. The job tiles were among the best-performing assets in the campaign, with reported engagement of around 39%.
