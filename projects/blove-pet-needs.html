Project Overview

K Pet Needs, a local pet supply store, needed a visual identity as a tribute to their late pets, Chocoby and Hunniby.

Tools

Challenge

Two beloved Frenchies passed away tragically from an accident. The visual identity needed to be carefully crafted to honor their memory while establishing a strong brand presence in the market.

Visual Identity + Logo Design

Concept - The dogs and pet owners' needs are in the hearts of the brand owner

Brand Renaming

Brand Colours

The brand colours were chosen to reflect the warmth and love associated with pets, while also conveying a sense of trust and reliability. The primary colors are soft and inviting, while the secondary colors provide contrast and versatility for various applications.

Typography

Montserrat was chosen for its clean and modern appearance, making it suitable for both digital and print applications. The font is versatile and legible, ensuring that the brand's messaging is clear and accessible across all platforms.

Key Takeaways

The brand continued to operate under the visual identity I designed for five years until it closed in 2026, which showed me how much the identity resonated with the owners and reflected the story they wanted to preserve. This project taught me that great branding is about more than aesthetics—it is about creating meaningful, lasting connections through thoughtful design. Although the business has since closed, its legacy lives on through its Instagram archive, preserving the brand and the memories behind it.
