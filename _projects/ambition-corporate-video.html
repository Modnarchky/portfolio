01

Asset Sourcing

I sourced suitable stock footages, visual assets, and music that supported the video's message while aligning with the brand's visual style. The selected footages were chosen to complement the existing content and improve the overall production value.

02

Compositing & Motion Graphics

Using Adobe After Effects, I composited the stock footages with the provided assets and created motion graphics, text animations, and visual transitions. I ensured that all elements were visually cohesive and reinforced the key messages throughout the video.

03

Final Edit

I refined the video by making final adjustments to timing, pacing, and overall flow, ensuring it effectively communicated the company's culture and values.
