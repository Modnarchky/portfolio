Visual Identity + Logo Design

Concept - The dogs and pet owners' needs are in the hearts of the brand owner

Brand Renaming

Brand Colours

The brand colours were chosen to reflect the warmth and love associated with pets, while also conveying a sense of trust and reliability. The primary colors are soft and inviting, while the secondary colors provide contrast and versatility for various applications.

Typography

Montserrat was chosen for its clean and modern appearance, making it suitable for both digital and print applications. The font is versatile and legible, ensuring that the brand's messaging is clear and accessible across all platforms.
